HOW TO PLAY

Take turns. Blow up every enemy caterpillar, or knock them into the water. Last team wiggling wins. Crates drop in now and then: grab them for health or extra weapons.

Online: one player picks HOST ONLINE, sets some teams to ONLINE PLAYER and opens a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE and type the room code, or just open the invite link.
