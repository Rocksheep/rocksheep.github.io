HOW TO PLAY

New to chess? Start with LEARN. Coach Fleecher walks you through every piece and rule with little puzzles, from how a rook moves to checkmating with two rooks.

Moving: click a piece, then click where it should go, or drag it there. Dots show where it can go; rings show what it can capture. Click an enemy piece to see where it could go next turn.

The coach: with COACH on, the sheep tells you why a move isn't allowed, warns you when a piece is in danger, and asks if you really meant a move that throws away material. HINT shows a good move and why. Pieces that can be captured for free look scared.

Winning: checkmate the enemy king: attack it so it has no way out. A game is a draw on stalemate (no legal move but not in check), the same position three times, 50 moves each without a capture or pawn move, or when nobody can ever checkmate.

Online: pick HOST ONLINE and open a room. Your friend picks JOIN ONLINE and types the room code, or opens the invite link. Anyone else who joins can watch.
