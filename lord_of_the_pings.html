HOW TO PLAY

Your workers mine gold from gold mines and chop wood from trees, and carry it back to your hall. Spend it on buildings and an army. Every unit eats food, so build food buildings (Farms, Pig Farms, Rat Hutches, Compost Heaps or Aphid Ranches, depending on your race) to feed more. Build a saw mill next to a forest so your workers don't have to walk so far with wood. Destroy every building of the other teams to win.

Flyers: every race can train one flying unit at its lab. Flyers go straight over trees, water and walls. Only ranged units, towers and other flyers can hit them, so keep some ranged units around.

Walls, siege and ultimates: workers can build walls; drag to lay a whole line. Siege engines from the workshop smash buildings and walls from far away. Each race also has one ultimate unit, summoned at its altar. You can only have one at a time.

Islands and boats: pick ISLANDS and every base starts on an island of its own. Land units can't swim, so build a shipyard on the shore. Its transports carry your army: right-click a transport with units selected to get them on board, then right-click land (or press U) to sail over and let them off. Warships fight at sea and shoot at the coast, battleships wreck buildings from the water. If a transport sinks, everyone on it goes down too.

Lemmings: their Builders can lay bridges across the water (drag for a line), every Lemming pops when it dies, and pressing D lights their fuse: five seconds later they go off. Oh no!

Online: pick HOST ONLINE, set some players to ONLINE PLAYER and open a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE, choose a race and type the room code, or open the invite link.
