HOW TO PLAY

Your workers mine gold from gold mines and chop wood from trees, and carry it back to your hall. Spend it on buildings and an army. Every unit eats food, so build food buildings (Farms, Pig Farms, Rat Hutches, Compost Heaps or Aphid Ranches, depending on your race) to feed more. Build a saw mill next to a forest so your workers don't have to walk so far with wood. Destroy every building of the other teams to win.

Flyers: every race can train one flying unit at its lab. Flyers go straight over trees, water and walls. Only ranged units, towers and other flyers can hit them, so keep some ranged units around.

Online: pick HOST ONLINE, set some players to ONLINE PLAYER and open a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE, choose a race and type the room code, or open the invite link.
