THE FIVE GODS

GOALS

◀ ARCADE

POLDERHEIMA FRISIAN CITY BUILDER

The year is 690. Found a town on the wild Frisian coast, raise sod huts into gabled townhouses, keep five old gods happy and hold back the North Sea.

HOW TO PLAY

Settlers walk in along the old road at the edge of the map. Lay roads from it and put house plots next to them; families will move in and build sod huts.

Everything in Polderheim is delivered on foot. Walkers leave their building and wander the roads: the market woman brings food, priests bring the gods, the fire watch puts out fires and the reeve collects taxes. A house only gets what walks past it.

Houses grow when they get more: water from a well, then food and a temple, then pottery, then two kinds of food, a mead hall, two gods and a nice neighbourhood, and finally cloth and three gods. Click a house to see what it wants next.

Food and goods go round on handcarts: farms and crafts send them to a storehouse, and the market fetches them from there.

The five gods, Wodan, Donar, Frija, Nehalennia and Fosite, each want temples as your town grows, and get jealous of each other. Happy gods send blessings, angry gods send disaster. A festival cheers one up quickly.

In autumn and winter, storm floods come off the sea. Low land floods first (VIEW: FLOODS shows it). Build dikes to keep the water out.
