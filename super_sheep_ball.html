HOW TO PLAY

Tilt the stage to roll your sheep to the GOAL as fast as you can. Every coin is worth bonus points. Fall off and you're put back at the last flag.

World 2, the Woolly Wonderworks, is a toy factory full of springs and trampolines, conveyor belts, sweeping bars, pistons, lifts, rocking planks, fans that blow you upward, tiles that blink away and loop-the-loops. Hit a loop fast!

Finished? Your camera follows whoever is still rolling; steer left or right to switch sheep. Friends who join in the middle of a race watch it and join from the next stage.

Racers never bump into each other. You see everyone else roll, and anyone close to you turns see-through so you always know which ball is yours.

Online: pick HOST ONLINE and open a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE and type the room code, or open the invite link.

Points: 1st 1000, 2nd 750, 3rd 550, 4th 400 and so on, plus 50 for every coin. When the first sheep reaches the goal, the rest have 20 seconds left.
