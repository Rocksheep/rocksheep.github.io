HOW TO PLAY

Race: first across the line after the last lap wins. Don't drive off the edge of the table! Yellow arrows give you a turbo, ramps make you jump.

Knockout: the camera follows whoever is in front. Fall off the screen and you're out. Last car left wins the point, and the first to the target wins the knockout.

Online: pick HOST ONLINE, set some racers to ONLINE PLAYER and open a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE and type the room code, or open the invite link.
