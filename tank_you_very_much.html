HOW TO PLAY

Goal: capture the enemy HQ or wipe out every unit they own. Armies on the same team win together. With a day limit on, whoever holds the most buildings when time runs out wins.

Turns: armies move one after the other; a full round is a day. At the start of your turn every building you own pays income, units parked on your buildings refuel, rearm and heal 2 HP (the repairs go on your bill), and aircraft and ships burn a bit of fuel. Press END TURN when you're done.

Capture: Rifles and Bazookas standing on a building can CAPTURE it. A building has 20 points and a squad takes off as many as it has HP each turn, so a healthy squad needs two turns. Move away and the count resets. Capturing an HQ knocks that army out of the war.

Build: click an empty base, airport or port you own. Bases make ground units, airports aircraft, ports ships and landers. A new unit can't move until next turn, so build at the end of your turn.

Direct and indirect: tanks, squads, jets and the like move and then fire at a neighbour, and the target shoots back with whatever HP it has left. Howitzers, MLRS, SAMs and Battleships fire from 2 to 6 tiles away and never get a reply, but they can't move and fire in the same turn. Terrain stars shave damage off; aircraft get none.

Fuel and ammo: most guns have limited ammo; when it runs out the unit falls back to its machine gun, if it has one. Aircraft and ships burn fuel every day and crash at zero. Park on a building or next to an APC to fill up.

Transports: the APC and the Lifter carry one squad, a Lander two ground units, a Cruiser two helicopters. Move onto the transport to board, pick UNLOAD to drop next to it. Landers only unload onto beaches, ports and land they're touching.

Fog of war: you only see what your units and buildings see. Woods and reefs hide whatever stands in them unless you're right next door. Running into an enemy you couldn't see stops you in your tracks. Dived subs vanish except from adjacent tiles.

CO powers: every army has a commanding officer with a quirk and a power. Dealing and taking damage charges the meter; when the stars are full, fire the power from the CO card or the menu. It lasts until the end of your turn.

Online: pick HOST ONLINE, set a seat to ONLINE PLAYER and open a room. Friends pick JOIN ONLINE and type the room code, or open the invite link. The host's screen runs the game; anyone who joins without a seat gets to watch.
